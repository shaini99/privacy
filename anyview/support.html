Anyview Support

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Anyview reads comics, books and documents — from your device, or straight from your own servers. No account, no ads, nothing collected.

Formats · Adding files · Book servers · Reading · Syncing your place · Common questions · Contact

Supported formats

  • Comics — zip, cbz, rar, cbr, 7z, cb7, tar, cbt
  • Single images — png, jpg, jpeg, gif, bmp, tif, tiff, webp, heic, heif
  • E-books — epub, mobi, azw, azw3, prc, fb2, fbz
  • Text and web pages — txt, html, htm, xhtml
  • Documents — pdf, hwp, hwpx

Archives are read as they are. You do not need to unzip anything first.

Getting files into the app

Wi-Fi Transfer

Open Wi-Fi Transfer in the app, then type the address it shows into a browser on your computer. Drag files onto the page. Your phone and computer must be on the same Wi-Fi network. The transfer server runs only while that screen is open and stops the moment you close it or the app goes to the background.

Other ways

  • Open a file from the Files app
  • Use Share from another app and choose Anyview
  • Copy a file and paste it into the library

Book servers

Anyview can read straight from servers you run yourself — a NAS, a shared folder on your computer, or a book server such as Komga, Kavita or Calibre-Web. Tap + at the bottom left of the library, choose the kind of server, and enter its address. A username and password are optional.

  • OPDS — the catalog address of your book server, e.g. http://192.168.0.10:25600/opds/v1.2/catalog for Komga or http://192.168.0.10:8083/opds for Calibre-Web. Kavita shows its OPDS address in your user settings.
  • SMB — a shared folder, e.g. smb://192.168.0.10/books. On a Mac, turn on File Sharing in System Settings › General › Sharing.
  • WebDAV — e.g. http://nas.local:5005/books
  • FTP, FTPS, SFTP — e.g. ftp://192.168.0.10/books, ftps://nas.example.com/books, sftp://192.168.0.10/books

Tap a book to read it right away without downloading it, or tap ⬇ to save it to your library. SMB, WebDAV, FTP, FTPS and SFTP can save a whole folder. In a server folder, tap ⋯ at the top right to sort by name, date modified or date read (OPDS catalogs keep the server’s order).

The first time you connect to a server on your home network, iOS asks for Local Network access. Please allow it — without it Anyview cannot reach the server. You can change it later in Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network.

Anyview connects only to the servers you add, directly from your device. Passwords are kept in the iOS Keychain.

Reading

Tap the center of the page to show the controls, then tap ⋯ at the bottom right for the reading menu.

  • Page Turn (in Themes & Settings) — Page Curl, Slide, Instant or Continuous Scroll
  • Auto-Turn — turns pages or scrolls on its own at the pace you set
  • Read Aloud — reads text and e-books aloud, keeps going in the background or on the Lock Screen, with a sleep timer
  • Reading Direction, Two-Page spreads and Split Pages for comics and manga

Syncing your place

If you are signed in to iCloud, your place in each book follows you to your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account.
If you have read the book more recently on another device, Anyview asks whether to continue from there.
A book you have not opened on this device yet simply opens at that place.
Only reading positions are synced — never the files themselves — and they are stored in your own iCloud.

The book has to be the same file on both devices.
Paged views split pages to fit each screen, so on another device the same sentence may be a little further down the page rather than at the top.

Common questions

A comic will not open

An archive needs to contain at least one image. Archives protected with a password are not supported. If the file opens correctly on your computer but not here, please email it to us — or describe how it was made — and we will look into it.

A text file shows garbled characters

Anyview detects common encodings, including the Korean ones, and converts them for you. If a file still looks wrong, saving it as UTF-8 on your computer will fix it. Tell us about the file and we will try to add support for it.

The paragraphs run together

Some text files break every visual line instead of every paragraph. Turn on Auto-Format Paragraphs in the viewer settings and the text will reflow properly.

Where did my place in the book go

Anyview remembers where you stopped in every book and returns you there when you open it again. With iCloud, it also follows you to your other devices — see Syncing your place.

Can I hide some folders

Yes. The hidden folder feature keeps chosen folders out of the library until you unlock them.

Can I use my own fonts

Yes. Add a font file the same way you add a book, and it appears in the font picker in the viewer.

Reading a manga right to left

Open the viewer settings and set the reading direction. You can also turn on two-page spreads, or split a wide double-page scan into two pages automatically.
