Anyview Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Anyview does not collect any personal information.

What we don’t collect

This app has no capability to communicate with external servers. It uses no analytics, no advertising, and no account system. The developer cannot access any of your information.

What is stored on your device

Books and fonts you add, your reading positions, bookmarks, and app settings are stored only on your device and never leave it.

Wi-Fi Transfer

This feature lets you exchange files with your own computer on the same Wi-Fi network. It runs only while you have the transfer screen open, and stops immediately when you close the screen or the app moves to the background. Files travel directly between your device and your computer, never through an external server.

Data deletion

Deleting the app removes all stored data.
